Well, what kind of math do you like? Pure math? Applied math?

There are tons of active areas of research in both. Active areas of pure math research is areas like algebraic geometry,
functional analysis(spectral theory, operator theory, operator algebras, etc.),
harmonic analysis,
analysis of PDEs,
abstract algebra(such as representation theory),
mathematical physics(can be argued as applied research too, same with PDEs),
combinatorics and graph theory,
number theory(algebraic, analytic, combinatorial, etc.),
etc.

Applied research is in areas like applied PDEs(free boundary problems, boundary layer, etc.), control theory, optimization(convex optimization, nonlinear programming, etc.), mathematical modeling (multiphysics and multiscale modeling, continuum modeling, etc.), numerical analysis(numerical linear algebra, Galerkin methods, etc.), computational fluid dynamics, mathematics of deep learning, algorithms and complexity theory, etc.
